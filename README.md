##  About MINDMATE PRO

*"It doesn't just check your answer. It understands your mistake."*

MINDMATE PRO is an AI-powered adaptive learning concept designed to solve a core problem in ed-tech: standard platforms give all students the exact same generic hints when they get a question wrong. MINDMATE PRO inspects line-by-line step reasoning to uncover the student's true **Mistake DNA** and delivers personalized, adaptive learning paths.

---

##  Key Features Implemented in Prototype

1. **The WOW Moment**: Interactive side-by-side demonstration showing Student A (Concept Gap) vs. Student B (Sign Confusion) receiving completely different adaptive learning paths for the exact same wrong answer (`x = -2, -3`).
2. **Diagnostic Pre-Test & AI Weak Area Predictor**: Pre-quiz simulator predicting weak areas using confidence ratings before teaching starts.
3. **Show Your Work Parser & Misconception Detector**: Derivation scratchpad pinpointing step-by-step errors line by line and classifying Mistake DNA.
4. **MindMate Socratic AI Chatbot**: Conversational Socratic AI tutor asking *"Why?"* and guiding students without spoiling direct answers.
5. **Personalized Learning Path**: Custom micro-lessons, visual trinomial sign rule guides, and zero-product property drills.
6. **Peer Learning Connector**: Matchmaker connecting students with peer helpers who recently mastered the exact misconception.
7. **Gamified Student Dashboard**: Skill mastery breakdown, Mistake DNA distribution, streak counter, and XP points.
8. **Teacher Intelligence Dashboard**: Class-wide analytics (48 students, 74% avg mastery), misconception breakdown, and auto-flagged student intervention protocols.
